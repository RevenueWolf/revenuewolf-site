# RevenueWolf Website Creative Brief

## Overview

Build a one-page marketing website for RevenueWolf — a full-service B2B marketing agency. The site is built on Next.js and deployed to Cloudflare Pages. The repo is already scaffolded and connected.

## Brand

- **Company:** RevenueWolf LLC
- **Founder:** Chris Cleland (Birmingham, Alabama)
- **Positioning:** Full-service B2B marketing agency — strategy AND execution. Not just consulting. Solo operator + curated subcontractors, powered by AI.
- **Voice:** "We" language. Present as a capable team, not a freelancer. Chris is the face and strategic lead; the brand is the operation.
- **Target:** B2B companies of all sizes — from founder-led startups to established companies modernizing their go-to-market.
- **Key differentiator:** Works directly with and for sales teams, not just marketing departments. Covers the full customer lifecycle from first touch to lifetime value. Strategy + execution under one roof.
- **Ethos:** Lift the team, replace the overhead. We make existing people better; we challenge bloated agency spend. We don't come in to cut headcount — we come in to make everyone more effective.

## Design Direction

- **Vibe:** Clean and minimal as the foundation, but with warmth. Professional white space and structure, conversational copy, warm color accents.
- **Tone:** "I'm an expert you'd actually enjoy working with." Not corporate sterile.
- **Color palette:** Red/black/gray brand palette. Warm accents.
- **Typography:** Distinctive, not generic. Avoid Inter, Arial, Roboto.

We built RevenueWolf differently.

**AI-powered execution.** AI has fundamentally changed what a lean team can deliver. We use it across content, research, automation, and reporting — not to replace thinking, but to move faster and smarter than a team twice our size.

**A curated network of specialists.** When your project needs a designer, a developer, or a copywriter, we bring in the right person from our network. You get the expertise without the overhead. We manage them. You never have to.

**Built to lift your team, not replace them.** If you have people, we make them better. Better tools, better processes, better strategy to work from. We're not here to take anyone's job. We're here to make everyone's job easier.
